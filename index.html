SOLE AUTHOR • ARCHIVAL CURATOR

Side Atelier

Architectural essayist, digital typographer, and founder of Atelier. Exploring the quiet intersection of cognitive restraint, classical book design, and humane software ergonomics.

Dividing working time between Copenhagen and Zurich, Side Atelier writes long-form monographs drafting on unbleached Japanese cotton paper before transferring prose to the digital folio. The work advocates for the complete elimination of advertising networks, clickbait, and algorithmic noise from human reading spaces.
